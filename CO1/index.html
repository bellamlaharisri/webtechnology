About the University

A multi-disciplinary campus built around six schools

Saveetha University is a deemed-to-be university located in Thandalam, Kancheepuram district, on the outskirts of Chennai, Tamil Nadu. The campus spreads across more than 180 acres and houses classrooms, research laboratories, libraries, hostels and sports facilities within a single green campus.

The university brings together constituent schools spanning medicine, dentistry, engineering, law, pharmacy, management, nursing and allied health sciences — allowing students from very different disciplines to study, and often collaborate, on one campus.

Its law school, Saveetha School of Law, was established in 2009 and holds a NAAC ‘A’ grade accreditation. Across the university, programmes are offered at undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, super-speciality and PhD levels, with flagship courses including MBBS, BDS, B.Tech, BA LLB / BBA LLB, LLM, MBA and a wide range of allied health sciences degrees.
